# confidence scoring

Four keys decide when a card returns.

After you flip a card, grade your own recall. The grade is the schedule. Weak cards circle back inside the same session until they stick.

1

Again

No recall. The card returns about three cards from now and drops back to the start of the ladder.

2

Hard

Shaky recall. The card returns about eight cards from now and holds its place on the ladder.

3

Good

Solid recall. The card climbs one rung. Climb high enough and it graduates out of the session.

4

Easy

Instant recall. The card jumps two rungs and retires immediately. Your progress ring fills as cards graduate.

# about this tool

Practice R in short, focused sessions.

A flashcard study tool for building confidence with R, RStudio, and the tidyverse.

Each deck focuses on a specific part of the R learning process, from setup and basic commands to data visualization, wrangling, importing, reshaping, modeling output, and text cleaning.

The tool uses confidence-based review. Cards you know well move out of the session. Cards that feel harder come back sooner, so practice time is spent where it is most useful.

I built this to give learners a low-pressure way to review R concepts, test their memory, and keep improving one card at a time.

It is especially useful for quick review before a class, workshop, project, or return to R after time away.
