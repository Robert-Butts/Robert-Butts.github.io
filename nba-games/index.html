NBA Game Explorer

Pick a team and one of its 2024-25 games to see the score difference after every scoring play, from the opening tip to the final buzzer. The season here includes 1,321 games: the regular season, the NBA Cup Championship, the Play-In Tournament, and the playoffs.

How to read it

The line

The line starts at zero at the opening tip. Each step up or down is one made basket or free throw. When the line is above the middle, the team you picked leads, and the higher it goes, the bigger the lead. The line takes the color of whichever team is ahead.

Lead changes and ties

A lead change happens when the lead passes from one team to the other, even if the score was tied in between. Taking the first lead of the game does not count. Times tied counts each time the score becomes tied after the opening tip.

Runs

A run is the points one team scores in a row before the other team scores. The chart shades every run of 8 or more points, and the summary shows each team's biggest run of any size.

Timeouts

Every quarter has two mandatory timeouts. Under NBA Rule 5, if no team calls a timeout before 6:59, the scorer takes one at the next dead ball and charges it to the home team. If no other timeout comes before 2:59, the scorer takes another and charges it to the team not yet charged.

The data does not say which timeouts were mandatory, so this page applies that rule to mark them. Hollow dots are mandatory timeouts. Solid dots are timeouts a team chose to call.

The data

ESPN's play-by-play and schedule data for the 2024-25 season, as collected by the SportsDataverse project, which also makes the hoopR package for R. The largest lead for each team matches ESPN's box scores in every game but two. In one of those games, ESPN's plays do not add up to the official score, and the page says so.
