About

I help people see what their data is saying. The thread through all of my work is the same: taking something complicated and making it clear. In my day job, that means helping faculty choose and use the right tools. On my own time, it means turning data into pictures that answer a question at a glance, and building small interactive tools that make learning stick.

I am strongly influenced by the Financial Times Visual Vocabulary, a guide that sorts charts by the question each one answers. It inspired my Chart Gallery.
