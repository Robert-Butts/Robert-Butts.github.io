About

Part educator. Part technologist. Always curious.

The thread through all of my work is the same: taking something complicated and making it clear. In my day job I help faculty choose and use the right technology. On my own time I turn data into pictures, and ideas into small tools that make learning stick.

I am always learning, and right now that means data analytics. I also enjoy basketball, video games, and a healthy amount of YouTube.
