About

Hi, I am Robert Butts.

I work at Denison University, where I help faculty, staff, and students use technology to teach more clearly, learn more confidently, and make better decisions.

The thread through all of my work is the same: taking something complicated and making it clear. In my day job that means helping faculty choose and use the right tools. On my own time it means turning data into pictures that answer a question at a glance, and building small interactive tools that make learning stick.

I am always learning. Right now I am deep in data analytics, looking for new ways to bring numbers into more parts of my work and my life. Away from the screen I enjoy basketball, video games, and watching the show Taskmaster with my wife.

What I do

A few ways I can help.

Design clear data visualizations and dashboards that a busy person can read in seconds.

Build small interactive tools, like the practice games on this site, that make learning stick.
