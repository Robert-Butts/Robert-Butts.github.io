The Chart Gallery.

58 chart types, grouped into nine families by the question each one answers. Every chart has notes on what it is best for and how to build it. Charts marked Full lesson also have a sample CSV file, R code, and real-world examples. Full lessons are added one family at a time.

Inspired by the Financial Times Visual Vocabulary. Read the credits

Credit where it is due

Built on the Financial Times Visual Vocabulary.

The nine families in this gallery come from the Visual Vocabulary, a poster and guide made by the Financial Times Visual Journalism team. It grew out of the Graphic Continuum by Jon Schwabish and Severino Ribecca. In 2018, Andy Kriebel rebuilt the whole guide in Tableau so anyone could explore every chart with real data.

Their work changed how I think about choosing a chart: start with the question, then find the family that answers it. This gallery would not exist without them.

This site is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Financial Times. The descriptions, sample data, charts, and code here are my own.
